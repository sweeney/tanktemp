## Adaptive cadence, issue #1 (2026-11-09)

The issue settled the rules, the curve and the contract. These are the
choices it left open, or that building it turned up:

| # | Guess | Why | To change |
|---|---|---|---|
| G38 | **The default trigger is 1.5 °C/min** (and see G51: at the fast interval it's effectively a step count). | The issue's measurements, 2 days at 30 s on 10 probes, as the largest per-probe rate in each sweep: boiler off p50 0.12, p90 0.50, p99 3.75 °C/min; heating p50 0.50, p90 2.00, p99 14.25. 1.5 catches the draws (inlet falls of 3–37 °C, outlet rises of 2–10 °C) and some of each reheat; 2.0 would fire in about 2.6% of quiet sweeps. It's a config key so it can be tuned from `/api/status`. **Confirmed on the rig (review on #4, 2026-11-10):** 23 h at 1.0 °C/min, replayed at five thresholds. At 1.5, every draw that 1.0 caught is caught, 0–30 s later, for about 7% fewer sweeps (3797 a day, against 2745 fixed at 30 s and 8235 fixed at 10 s); what it gives up is slow warming under about 1.5 °C/min (one heating-only call), and a shared reheat entered 7 min later. At 2.0 a morning reheat is caught 24 min late; at 3.0, 6 small tap-run draws are missed. Below 1.0 adds sweeps and catches nothing more. No spell was held open by flicker at 1.0 (G51) in 23 h. | `sweep.fast.trigger_c_per_min`; the default is `config.DefaultFast` |
| G39 | **The default hold is 2m and decay 5m**, fast 10s. | The issue's numbers: 33 sweeps in the 8 minutes after one event, against 16 at a fixed 30 s. `TestTheIssuesWorkedExample` pins that. | `config.DefaultFast` |
| G40 | **The two-step minimum (0.125 °C) is a constant, not a config key.** | It's the sensor's step size, which no rig changes; one more knob would only invite a value under one step. | `cadence.MinChangeC` |
| G41 | **`fast.interval` must be at least 2 s**, as well as the G6 minimum. | `timestamp` is to the second and a repeated one means a replay (WIRE.md). | G42 | **Cadence log lines start `cadence:`**, not `sweep:` as the issue's example did, and name the probe `tank/position` like the other journal lines. `/api/status` names it `device_id.position`, the statehouse primary, as the issue asked. | `^sweep ` is the readings and `^sweep:` the faults (G29, G20); a third kind under `sweep:` would muddle both greps. | `cadence.Decision.Line` |

| G45 | ~~Rates use the sweeps' wall-clock timestamps.~~ **Retired by review on #4: the cadence runs on the monotonic clock.** Rates and the curve use each sweep's start as lifecycle hands it to `Work` (`time.Now`, monotonic), so a wall-clock step can't cause a spurious or missed trigger or hold fast. `Sweep.At`, the wall clock, is still what's published, and labels the reported trigger. | G46 | **The next sweep is due one interval after the previous one was due**, not after it actually started (the issue said "after the previous one started"). | G51 | **At the fast interval, the trigger is in practice a step count, so staying fast is easier than going fast.** Found on the rig (review on #4, a reheat at 1.0 °C/min): at a 10 s gap, 1.0 °C/min is 0.167 °C, three DS18B20 steps, and every hold-extending trigger was exactly 1.0 or 1.1. On a noisier probe, three-step jitter could hold fast indefinitely. If that's seen: keep `trigger_c_per_min` at 1.5 or more with a 10 s fast interval (4 steps), or raise `MinChangeC` (G40), or make the minimum change scale with the gap. | `sweep.fast.trigger_c_per_min`, `cadence.MinChangeC` |
